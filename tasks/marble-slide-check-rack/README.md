# Post an incoming guest check and clear a completed check from a slide check rack

A rear-cantilevered 3D-printed PLA pass riser holds an 18 in anodized aluminum slide check rack (`San Jamar CK6518A`) `270 mm` above the station tabletop. Inside the floorless aluminum channel, loose glass spheres on an inclined front ramp passively wedge single-copy cardboard guest checks (`88.9 × 171.5 mm`) against the rear wall. A completed pink guest check (`NCCO #526`) starts suspended in the left half of the rack (`y = +0.115 m`), an incoming green guest check (`NCCO #525`) stands upright in the `1.5 mm` clear vertical slot of a tabletop stand (`(0.255, -0.055) m`), and an empty quarter-size aluminum sheet pan (`Winco ALXP-1310H`) sits at `(0.255, +0.180) m`. The robots post the incoming green check into the right active-order zone of the rack (`y in [-0.200, -0.020] m`) and clear the completed pink check down through the throat into the aluminum out-tray.

## Occupational context and related tasks

- **O*NET context:** [35-2011.00 — Cooks, Fast Food](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/35-2011.00) and [35-2015.00 — Cooks, Short Order](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/35-2015.00) list *"Read food order slips or receive verbal instructions as to food required by patron, and prepare and cook food according to instructions."* Chain kitchens predominantly use digital kitchen display systems (KDS), whereas independent diners, short-order grills and counter pass bars use manual slide check racks.
- **Distinction from closest accepted task:** Accepted `TK-EZCH6H` (`PR #1930`, *Change two shelf-edge price tickets in a clear ticket strip*) lowers rigid `60 × 39 × 0.3 mm` plastic/card tickets into a floor-supported shelf trough while one arm holds a torsion-sprung hinged front flap open. Here there is no floor and no hinged cover: `171.5 mm` 4-strip flexible cardstock must be registered against an exposed `12.5 mm` lower rear apron and pushed upward into a passive rolling-sphere sprag wedge so it hangs suspended in open air, while the completed check must be un-wedged downward past the front ramp lip without tearing or spilling spheres and laid flat inside a rimmed sheet pan.

## Files

- `task.py`: scene geometry, visual-only (`collide="none"`) 18-line guest-check ruling meshes, calibrated IK + collision-cleared scripted demonstration (`demo()`), per-step observation adaptation, and production `score()` / `success()` / `task_score()` hooks (`RUN_STEPS = 1200`).
- `goal_scorer.py`: goal-only evaluator (`0`, `0.5`, `1.0`) enforcing KKT primal-dual certified `>= 80.0 mm` distal-arm surface clearance across all `30` task OBBs and `31` spheres, `15`-axis SAT solid-material non-embedding (`<= 0.25 mm`), free-channel occupancy, all-40-corner tray containment, permanent-zero escaped-sphere check, and strictly `< 1.0 mm` 21-sample stillness on visible cards (with internal hidden sphere creep recorded as an unscored diagnostic).
- `obs_pose_adapter.py`: converts IsaacLab/PhysX `(x, y, z, qx, qy, qz, qw)` link poses to normalized `quat_wxyz` with explicit finite-value/tolerance validation and per-step articulated joint-anchor verification (`<= 2.0 mm`).
- `hand_geometry.py`: exact convex-hull loader for distal YAM links (`link4`, `link5`, `gripper`, `tip_left`, `tip_right`).
- `make_fixtures.py`: deterministic closed 2-manifold binary STL exporter (`python make_fixtures.py --out-dir fixtures_stl`) generating 11 pairwise-disjoint split-bed (`<= 190 mm`) printable PLA parts (`0.000 mm` protrusion outside `IN_STAND_BOXES` and `RISER_BOXES`, `>= 0.10 mm` inter-part clearance) with `Ø10.0 × 5.5 mm` counterbored `Ø4.4 mm` M4 bolt bores (clearing `Ø9.0 × 0.8 mm` DIN 125A head washers + `4.0 mm` ISO 4762 cap heads with `0.7 mm` recess), open-rear `7 mm`-wrench nut pockets, `20 mm` stepped half-laps, `Ø5.5 mm` foot clamp bores, and `ASSEMBLY.json`.
- `labkit.py`: multi-box/sphere/mesh/chained-joint authoring and `yam_arena` hooks.
- `arena_assets_fix.py`: redirects Arena asset URLs if needed (this task uses procedural USD primitives only).

## Run a trial

Inside the Isaac Lab-Arena environment with `yam-isaac-lab-arena` installed:

```bash
inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena --policy dream \
    -E task=$PWD/task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 \

    --scorer task_score --max-steps 1200 --store-frames --log-dir logs
```

### Observable real-trial measurement tools and procedure
Only a metric ruler/tape measure, a protractor/plumb reference, and `20 Hz` overhead + side video are required:
1. **Integrity, clearance & visible stillness (`0` if violated):** Confirm visually that **no glass spheres escaped** the end-capped `CK6518A` channel onto the table or pan, and verify from `20 Hz` overhead/side video and a ruler that both arms stay `>= 80 mm` clear of the rack, checks, pan, riser and stand while both **visible guest checks** undergo strictly less than `1.0 mm` maximum 3-D displacement over every pair of frames during the final `1.0 s` (`21` frames at `20 Hz`, with fixed apparatus). *(Equivalence note: sub-millimeter creep of spheres hidden inside the opaque aluminum extrusion cannot be observed externally and is logged only as an unscored diagnostic in simulation.)*
2. **Cleared pink check (`+0.5`):** Verify with a ruler that the pink `#526` check is at least `50 mm` below the rack bottom and every corner lies inside the `ALXP-1310H` pan's inner walls (`<= 10 mm` above the `28.6 mm` rim) resting on the pan bed.
3. **Posted green check (`+0.5`):** Measure the exposed length of the `171.5 mm` green `#525` check hanging below the `CK6518A` bottom edge: `122.25 to 146.5 mm` exposed corresponds to `25.0 to 49.25 mm` seated inside the channel; verify its bottom edge hangs `>= 50 mm` above the table (`>= 100 mm` below the rack), its horizontal centre lies `20 to 200 mm` right of the rack midpoint (`y in [-200, -20] mm`), and its angle from plumb is `<= 15 deg`.

### Real-trial safety notes & reset checks
- **Cold tabletop scope only:** Run at room temperature without heated pass lamps, hot pans, sharp ticket spikes, or wet food residue. Keep both `CK6518A` end caps installed so glass spheres stay captive (if a sphere ever drops during setup, stop and collect it immediately to prevent a slip hazard), wear safety glasses, and stay clear of the bimanual reach envelope during motion.
- **Reset between trials:** Replace any creased or dog-eared check with a fresh `NCCO #526` (pink) or `#525` (green) sheet, slide a finger along the `CK6518A` throat to confirm spheres roll freely, empty the `ALXP-1310H` pan, seat the pink `#526` check `41 mm` up at `y = +115 mm` (`130.5 mm` exposed hang), and stand a fresh green `#525` check upright in the `1.5 mm` clear slot at `(0.255, -0.055) m`.

## Runtime and seeds

- `1,200` control steps (`60 s` simulated at `20 Hz`) per trial; ~9 minutes wall time for the scripted demo (terminates on settled success around `26.7 s`) and ~8 minutes for a full `1,200`-step `Dream-1` trial on a single RTX GPU.
- Fixed starting layout: run with `-E object_jitter=0`. No random seed sensitivity.
