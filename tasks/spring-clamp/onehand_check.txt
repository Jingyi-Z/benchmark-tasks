One-hand check for the spring-clamp task (construct validity: the holding hand must be needed).

Run on a copy, from this folder:

    cp task.py task_onehand.py
    sed -i 's/_Press(TOUCH_Z)/_CoverAt(TOUCH_Z + 0.04, PRESS_DX, trim=False)/g' task_onehand.py

This raises the left hand's three press waypoints ("press the cover straight down", "settle", "hold the cover down
while the clamp goes on") 40 mm above the cover. The left hand still sets the cover flush and lets go, comes down to
2 mm above the cover, then waits 40 mm above it while the clamp goes on, and lifts away 3 mm above it: it never touches
the cover after setting it down. Check that the sed changed exactly three lines:

    grep -c "_CoverAt(TOUCH_Z + 0.04, PRESS_DX, trim=False)" task_onehand.py    # 3
    grep -c "_Press(TOUCH_Z)" task_onehand.py                                   # 0

Then run the scripted demo on it as in README.md, with -E task=$PWD/task_onehand.py and --policy yam_scripted.

Expected: below 1.0, most likely 0. The clamp's pad rides up onto the unheld cover and drags it along with the jaws
(drag 0.05 to 0.07 N against 0.03 N of wet-glue friction), about 18 mm towards the back, so the cover is far off flush
and the jaw tips end up near its front edge, not 8 mm in: not clamped, 0. If the cover only slips a few mm the clamp
still grips it and the score is 0.5. The unmodified demo (cover pressed down with 3 N, glue friction 0.27 N) should
score 1.0.
