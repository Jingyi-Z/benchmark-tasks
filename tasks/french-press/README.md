# Fit, plunge, and turn the safety lid closed on a filled French press

Brace the handle of a loose `0.795 kg` filled `0.5`-litre (`4`-cup) Bodum Chambord cylindrical French press carafe (`96 mm` OD x `125 mm` glass beaker, `166 mm` overall width with handle, `190 mm` pressed overall height) on its cork coaster with one hand while the other hand picks up the `0.228 kg` plunger-lid assembly by its central guide collar from the drip stand, seats it coaxially onto the carafe rim with the one-piece safety lid rotated `43.15 to 180 degrees` (`~95.8 degrees` in the scripted demo) so its continuous `294.7-degree` solid pilot skirt completely occludes the `-Y` pour spout (turning the lid closed either before or after pressing is valid per the Bodum Chambord manual), and presses the seated plunger smoothly down (`<= 110 mm/s`, accumulating `>= 52 mm` of net downward stroke while seated on the carafe; pre-pressing on the stand or off-carafe earns no pressing credit, and filter entry/descent inside the `78.7 mm` filled brew bore above `110 mm/s` or seated plunger speed above `110 mm/s` latches `0.0`) through joint friction and viscous filtration damping (dimensionless legacy PhysX `jointFriction = 2.4` + `85 N s/m`). External carafe, whole-lid spout-closure topology, and assembled press dimensions follow official Bodum Chambord `0.5 L` (`1924-16` / `1928-57` manual / spare beaker `1504-10`) specifications; internal wall/skirt/dome/filter dimensions (`3 mm` glass wall, `82 mm` pilot skirt with `65.3-degree` `-Y` inner pour slot, `68 mm` stepped dome crown, `78 mm` filter disc, `22 mm` central collar, `36 mm` press knob), component sub-masses (`345 g` empty carafe, `148 g` safety lid, `80 g` plunger), and plunger joint parameters (dimensionless legacy `jointFriction = 2.4` + `85 N s/m` damping, not calibrated in absolute newtons) are engineering estimates.

## Real-Trial Bill of Materials, Preparation, Spill Protection, and Reset

- **Off-the-shelf & printable bill of materials:**
  - **French press:** Bodum Chambord `4`-cup (`0.5 L`) French press (`model 1924-16`, spare borosilicate beaker `1504-10`, `~USD 35 to 45`).
  - **Cork coaster:** Standard `5`-inch (`127 to 128 mm` OD x `6 mm`) round cork trivet/coaster (`~USD 3`, fixed at `x = 235 mm, y = +25 mm` with removable double-sided tape).
  - **Drip stand (`132 mm` base, `68 mm` cradle):** Off-the-shelf `5.2`-inch (`132 mm`) stainless lid/utensil rest (`~USD 8 to 12`) or a simple 1-piece 3D-printed PLA/PETG cradle (`132 mm` OD x `8 mm` base disc + concentric `86 mm` ID / `94 mm` OD open cylindrical ring `60 mm` tall = `68 mm` overall height, `< USD 2` filament, fixed at `x = 245 mm, y = -165 mm` with double-sided tape).
  - **Consumables:** Standard coarse-ground roasted coffee (`0.85 to 1.0 mm` French-press grind, `~USD 10 to 14/kg` or `~USD 0.30` per `25 g` trial) and potable room-temperature tap/filtered water.
- **Shared-rig spill protection:** Before physical trials, seal tabletop seams, cable pass-throughs, and robot arm base mounts with flush tape-down waterproof polyethylene barrier film (`<= 0.2 mm` thick, wiped dry so the `128 mm` cork coaster and `132 mm` drip stand sit at their nominal tabletop height and friction), place absorbent spill pads around the outer perimeter (`>= 150 mm` outside the coaster, drip stand, and arm sweep), and keep lint-free shop towels beside the station.
- **Safe room-temperature coffee and water preparation (each trial):** Always use **room-temperature potable water at `20 to 25 deg C` (`68 to 77 deg F`, verified by thermometer; never hot or boiling water, avoiding scald, glass thermal-shock, and steam hazards)**. Off-station, dose **`25.0 +/- 0.5 g`** of fresh dry coarse-ground roasted coffee (`0.85 to 1.0 mm` French-press grind; never reuse spent grounds) into the clean, dry `0.5 L` (`345 g`) Bodum Chambord beaker, add **`425 mL` (`425.0 +/- 5.0 g`)** of `20 to 25 deg C` water (`795 g` total filled carafe mass, `~450 cm^3` submerged brew-column volume / `78.7 mm` undisturbed center fill height), stir gently for **`5 seconds`** with a plastic or wooden spoon (never metal on borosilicate glass), and let the uncovered carafe **hydrate undisturbed for `4.0 minutes` (`240 +/- 15 s`)**. Wipe the beaker exterior dry, center the carafe upright on the cork coaster (`handle +Y, pour spout -Y`), and begin the trial within `60 s`.
- **Real-trial video and simple-tool scoring:** In physical evaluation, spills over the spout or rim, liquid blow-by splashing out of the spout during fast filter entry or pressing, and hard drops onto the tabletop or floor are judged directly from ordinary trial video (with the simulation's `38.9/50.3 deg`, `110 mm/s`, and `0.50 m/s` thresholds serving as proxies). Coaxial lid seating, spout occlusion by the solid stainless pilot skirt, `>= 52 mm` seated plunger stroke (knob/stem height measured before and after pressing), `>= 80 mm` hand clearance, and `1.0 s` final stillness are checked with a ruler/tape measure and trial video.
- **Between-trial handling and reset:** After final scoring, carry the carafe and plunger-lid assembly off-station to a sink prep counter before opening. Pull the plunger stem slowly up, lift out the plunger-lid, pour the `20 to 25 deg C` liquid through a fine mesh strainer (`<= 0.5 mm` screen), and discard all spent coffee grounds into a lined wet-waste bin (**never flush loose grounds down an unstrained drain**). Rinse the beaker, frame, safety lid, spiral plate, and stainless mesh filter clean under `20 to 25 deg C` tap water, towel-dry every surface thoroughly (`keeping the cork coaster and gripper pads dry`), inspect and wipe the station barrier sheet dry, pull the plunger stem fully up to its `0 mm` top stop, place the plunger-lid upright on the drip stand (`dark pour-slot mark facing -Y`), and prepare a fresh `25.0 g` + `425 mL` (`20 to 25 deg C`) batch hydrated `4.0 minutes` for the next trial.
