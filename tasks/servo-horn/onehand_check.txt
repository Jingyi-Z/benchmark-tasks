One-hand variant of the scripted demo, for checking in simulation that the link has to be held.

Run in this folder, on a copy of task.py (it edits the file in place):

    sed -i 's/^BLOCK_TOUCH = TOP + BLOCK_H + TIP_ABOVE_TCP  #/BLOCK_TOUCH = TOP + BLOCK_H + TIP_ABOVE_TCP + 0.06  #/' task.py

It raises every left-hand waypoint over the block (hover, touch and hold) by 6 cm, so the left hand comes no closer
than about 5 cm to the link (offline plan check: left to block 52.7 mm at its lowest) and never touches it. The right
hand's demo is unchanged. Then run the trial with --policy yam_scripted as in README.md.

Expected: below 1.0. Unheld, a vertical press at the shaft tips the link at 0.8 N, and even with the hub held
sideways by the pressing hand the link tips or its upright's edge slides above about 2.2 N (2.9 N with a bench
friction of 0.7), short of the 5 N the press fit needs. So the collar never latches: the horn ends at best on the
shaft but not seated (0.25), or off it (0.0). The link should be seen tipping towards the servo during the press and
rocking back onto its foot once the horn is let go.
