Unlock the captive head of a new liquid-soap pump and leave it raised on the upright bottle.

Run from this folder inside Isaac Lab-Arena with yam-isaac-lab-arena installed:

    inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena --policy yam_scripted -E task=$PWD/task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 --instruction "Unlock the soap pump and leave the bottle upright with its head raised, ready to press." --scorer task_score --max-steps 800 --store-frames --log-dir logs/demo

Replace yam_scripted with dream for the model trial. Bench-kit: DEMO_STEPS=800 ~/bench-kit/scripts/rc_run.sh <task-folder> demo; omit demo for the full demo/model pipeline. Fixed layout, object_jitter=0, no random seed. Script16.3seconds plus settling; trial40seconds at20Hz. Demo about2minutes wall-clock; model runtime depends on service latency and shared GPU load.

The two handled components are a loose0.55kg filled bottle/closure and its27g captive stem/head. They are an articulation, with the closure fused to the body and a passive axial spring and rotary head. Two lugs are physically held by continuous shoulders until they rotate into escape channels. The200N/m spring target is explicitly applied each step. Native absolute friction efforts are0.025Nm angular and0.05N linear; legacy jointFriction is0. No object-state controller reads, attachments, object-pose writes, injected turning effort or synthetic unlock flags. The fixed demo counterholds low on the bottle and rotates the head90degrees before releasing it. PUMP_DEBUG=1 prints scorer/body diagnostics only.

The end-state scorer measures head rise relative to the bottle, supported upright posture, all body/stem/head geometry stillness over21 samples and true object-surface distance from independent finger endpoints/midpoints. Numeric conditions are in task.json. Whole-body motion cannot substitute for relative rise, but harmless movement and holding the head while turning the body are permitted. There is no arbitrary bottle-rotation violation.

Fidelity: representative dimensions, material friction0.45, masses,18mm spring travel and force parameters are estimates. Native distributed head friction approximates elastic detents; closure unscrewing, PET flex, liquid flow and sealing are omitted. Each contact patch is conservatively bounded by half the smallest collider extent, capped at18mm; small shapes have zero forced minimum, with1mm maxima on the lugs/shoulders.
